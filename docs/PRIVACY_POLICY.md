# Privacy Policy - MyFamilyHealth

**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Effective Date:** [Date of Implementation]

## Introduction

MyFamilyHealth ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains our online information practices and the choices you can make about how your information is collected and used.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- Account registration data (name, email, phone, date of birth)
- Health records and medical documents
- Diagnostic scans and lab reports
- Appointment information and notes
- Family member health information

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Device information and IP address
- Browser type and usage data
- Session and cookie information

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Maintain and improve the Services
- Provide customer support
- Ensure platform security
- Comply with legal requirements
- Generate analytics (aggregated, de-identified data only)

**We do NOT:**
- Sell your personal or health data
- Share records without your consent
- Use health data for marketing without opt-in
- Allow third-party tracking

---

## 3. Data Security

- All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
- Encryption at rest for stored data
- Access controls and authentication required
- Regular security audits and monitoring

---

## 4. Your Privacy Rights

### GDPR (EU Users)
- Right to access your data
- Right to export your data
- Right to delete your account
- Right to data portability

### CCPA (California Users)
- Know what data we collect
- Delete your personal data
- Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- Request non-discrimination for exercising rights

### HIPAA (US Healthcare Users)
If you require HIPAA coverage, contact hipaa@myfamilyhealth.app to execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

---

## 5. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** Health records retained for account duration
- **Deleted accounts:** Data removed within 30 days
- **Backups:** Purged within 60 days
- **Legal holds:** May be retained if required by law

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MyFamilyHealth is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we'll promptly delete it.

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

We use:
- **Firebase (Google):** Cloud hosting, authentication, database
- **Analytics (Optional):** De-identified usage data only

---

## 8. Data Breach Notification

In case of a breach:
1. We investigate within 24 hours
2. Notify you via email (within 72 hours per GDPR)
3. Provide information on breach and protective steps
4. May offer complimentary credit monitoring

**Report a Breach:** security@myfamilyhealth.app
